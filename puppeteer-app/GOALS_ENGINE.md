# Combined Goals Engine

One engine selects exactly one future fixture/market per round from Over 2.5, Under 2.5, Over 3.5 and Under 3.5 Goals. All four markets use engine ID `goals`, one statistics record and one seven-bet Martingale cycle. Changing the market or goal line does not reset that cycle. It never issues simultaneous opposing choices for a round. Actual paired bookmaker quotes are required for each available goal line; both prices must exceed one, with no minimum odds of two.

The learner uses normalized bookmaker probabilities plus shrunk, recency-weighted league/probability-band and team forecast errors, fitted separately at the two goal boundaries inside one goals engine. Earlier chronological calibration can reduce the adjustment to zero when it worsens log loss. Same-fixture estimates are reconciled so P(4+ goals) cannot exceed P(3+ goals), and Under is the complementary probability. A total of three goals wins Over 2.5 and Under 3.5; four goals wins both Overs.

The engine compares estimated return (`learned probability * actual decimal odds - 1`) across the four markets and all valid fixtures, with winning probability and canonical fixture/market identity as deterministic tie breakers. This lets all four markets compete; ranking solely by win probability would always favor Over 2.5 over Over 3.5, and Under 3.5 over Under 2.5 on the same fixture. A higher payout alone does not establish an edge. This is a raw paper selection, including when the best estimated return is negative; it does not claim a profitable or officially qualified bet.

Choices, quoted price, probability, model recipe and calibration summaries are immutable before kickoff in `ml_pick_observations` and `goals_engine_decisions`. `goals_market_candidates` freezes the eligible fixture universe at each quoted line, including unselected markets, so later learning is not restricted to chosen winners. Learning deduplicates correlated observations and excludes unavailable/future/same-kickoff results, late captures and other leagues. Earlier settled 2.5/3.5 observations and old 3.5 fixture snapshots can seed learning, but that seed is not untouched historical profitability audit evidence.

Exact live/archive fixture identity settles snapshots and the selected market. Missing or conflicting results remain pending. The new engine is a prospective experiment with `publish_eligible=0`; it cannot automatically send real bets, Telegram messages or official signals. Existing publication and funded qualification gates remain unchanged. Unlimited capital is a research replay option, never proof of funded profitability.

The former `over35` and `under35` writers are disabled, and their observations remain in Archive with their original independent accounting. They are not merged or relabelled into the new engine's performance. New-system summaries use only the new combined goals trial alongside the original Safest, High Chance and AI Best engines. No global tracking reset is needed or performed.
