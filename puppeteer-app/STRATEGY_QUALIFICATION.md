# Independent 2x / 3x qualification

The user accepts either Martingale 2x or Martingale 3x passing, but only that specific mode qualifies. Both use NGN100,000 initial capital, NGN60 base stake and seven TOTAL bets per losing cycle (base is bet 1), followed by a reset. A win resets that engine's cycle. Qualification uses funded capital only, without credit or replenishment.

For each engine and current model recipe/version, a mode needs BOTH its own untouched chronological historical audit and its own genuine pre-kickoff forward evidence. Each side needs at least 100 settled picks across five distinct WAT days, positive net profit, a positive day-cluster profit lower bound and no unfunded next stake. A positive 3x sample cannot qualify 2x. A 2x historical pass plus a 3x forward pass qualifies neither. Flat stake and 1.5x remain comparisons, not qualification alternatives.

strategy_evidence.js exposes strategyQualification per mode, qualifiedStakingModes, separate twoXProfitabilityProven / threeXProfitabilityProven fields, and profitabilityProven when either mode passes. The dashboard shows both mode verdicts independently of the selected comparison. Choosing experimental observations for display does not qualify a policy rejected by the engine's existing selection rules.

Derived strategy_gate files store separate historical/forward results under each mode. strategy_release.releaseStatus accepts an explicit mode as its fourth argument; getModelStatus accepts a staking mode as its second argument. Legacy proof can only be interpreted as 2x and must include the correct capital, base stake, mode and cap. Wrong-version or older-than-six-hour evidence fails closed. Existing publication callers default to 2x; a 3x qualification cannot silently authorize that 2x path. No automatic stake-mode switching, real betting or Telegram dispatch is introduced by this change. Existing model, drift, policy and EdgeGate checks remain required.

The combined Goals Engine is included in evidence reporting. Its experimental forward choices are shown in the experimental lane. Until a valid untouched historical audit and a qualified-policy cohort exist, both qualification verdicts remain false. Archived broad specialist and separate-side goals trials cannot be relabelled into combined goals performance. Existing observations, tracking epochs and losing evidence are preserved.
